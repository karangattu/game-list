Education & Games May 2026 · Updated Nov. 2026

Small games, real stakes

The arcade has 13 browser games. The choices you make in them come from the work of birders and volunteers around San Francisco Bay.

Bird habitats

Sole Clue

You see a drawing of a bird's feet and choose one of four habitats. The answer shows the bird in its habitat. A mallard, for example, uses its webbed feet to paddle across a pond.

Birds

Sight, sound, and food

In Birdle and Birdle: After Dark, you identify birds by sight and sound. Marsh Madness gives you a spotting scope to find 12 birds. In Keeping Up with the Kestrels, you count raptors as they fly past. What's on the Menu? asks you to match birds to the food they eat.

Marsh habitat

Rail Refuge: High Tide Rising

A king tide floods the marsh. You have a limited supply of seeds to plant cover for endangered Ridgway's rails. The cover gives the birds a route to higher ground.

Marsh plants

Plant or Pull

Each card shows a plant you can find in a Bay Area marsh. You swipe right to plant a native species and left to pull an invasive one. Each card has a hint you can read before you decide.

Newts

Crossing Alma Bridge Road

In Newt Crosser, you play a newt and dodge cars as you cross the road. Save the Newts shows how volunteers can change the survival rate. In Save the Newts 3D, you take a flashlight out at night and rescue newts yourself.

Community

A shift at Hope's Corner

Hope's Corner serves free meals and offers showers and laundry in Mountain View. In Pantry to Plate, you sort donated food, cook meals, and serve guests. In Shower & Supplies, you assign showers, do laundry, and hand out supplies.

The tally in Newt Crosser means more after you try to get a newt past a truck. Newts make that crossing on Alma Bridge Road each winter.
